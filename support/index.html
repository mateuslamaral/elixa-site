Support & Contact

Need help with Elixa, found a bug, or have a privacy question? We’d love to hear from you.

Email: mateusl.amaral@gmail.com

Before you write

To help us reproduce an issue quickly, it’s useful to include:

  • Your device model and operating-system version.
  • The app version (shown in the in-game Settings).
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.

Common questions

How do I reset my progress?

Open the in-game Settings and use Reset progress. This clears all on-device data. Uninstalling the game also removes everything.

Do you collect my personal data?

No. Elixa has no accounts, no ads, and collects no personal information — only anonymous, aggregate gameplay analytics. See the Privacy Policy.

Is there a cost or subscription?

Elixa is designed as a pay-once game — one unlock removes ads and opens the full collection. No subscriptions. (The game is in closed testing and not yet publicly available.)
