What is Elixa

Sort the elements. Find some quiet.

Tap a tube, tap another, and the top ores pour across — stacking colour on colour until every tube holds one clean element. No timers. No lives. No energy that runs out. Just you, the ores, and as many unhurried minutes as you'd like.

What makes it different

Built for calm, not for clicks

Play at your pace

A special colourless ore pours freely into any tube — then transmutes, crystallising into a true element as it settles into place. A small moment of alchemy when you need it.

Flawless runs

Clear a level with the essential moves alone — no hints, no undo — and earn a Flawless: a perfect transmutation, marked with its own crimson stars.
