What is Elixa

Sort the elements. Find some quiet.

Tap a tube, tap another, and the top ores pour across — stacking colour on colour until every tube holds one clean element. No timers. No lives. No energy that runs out. Just you, the ores, and as many unhurried minutes as you’d like.

Built for calm, not for clicks

The theme is the logic

Alchemy isn’t a coat of paint. The seven elements are the colours you sort, transmutation is a real move, and the four chapters are your arc — the story and the rules are the same thing.

Transmute, don’t just sort

A prima-materia wildcard ore pours into any tube, then permanently transmutes into a true element the moment it settles. A move the sort genre simply doesn’t have.

Made to be felt

Every element bursts with its own effect, the haptics are tuned to the moment, and the music answers the difficulty. Satisfying by design, not by accident.

Readable by design

Each element ore has its own sculpted shape, not just a colour — with optional symbols and colourblind-friendly palettes for cues you can always trust.

Nothing gets in your way

No energy, no lives, no timers. Undo and restart are free and unlimited — the game never charges you for a mistake or makes you wait to play.

Mastery, not just completion

Stars are graded against the solver’s own optimal solution — and clearing a board with no hints or undo earns a Flawless. Every level is solver-verified, so the challenge is real, never random.
